About /

Located in the heart of Buenos Aires' historic La Boca district, Howard Johnson Undici República de La Boca is a premier 4-star destination just steps from the iconic La Bombonera stadium. This upscale property blends urban culture with luxury, featuring curated amenities such as hypoallergenic rooms, an outdoor pool, and a full-service spa.

With its focus on personalized guest experiences and modern comforts, the hotel stands as a contemporary landmark in one of the city's most vibrant neighborhoods.
