[005] FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything about how Barrow drafts, checks and keeps your data safe. Still curious?

Reach out↗
01What is Barrow?

An AI platform built for environmental professionals that drafts full-length environmental reports from raw site assessment data, starting with Phase I ESAs.

02What’s a Phase I ESA?

A Phase I Environmental Site Assessment evaluates a property for potential contamination and recognized environmental conditions (RECs), following the ASTM E1527-21 standard. It’s a core part of real estate due diligence.

03What documents do I need to upload?

Your usual source materials: EDR reports, historical aerials, Sanborn maps, site photos and assessment notes. Upload them directly or connect Google Drive or OneDrive.

04How long does a draft report take?

Work that usually takes weeks is drafted in hours, depending on the volume of source documents.

05How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your report volume and team. Reach out and we’ll put together a plan that fits your workflow.

06Are the draft reports industry compliant?

Drafts are structured around ASTM methodology. As with any draft, your Environmental Professional reviews and finalizes the report.

07Can I use my own template?

Yes. Reports can be arranged into your company’s forms so clients and jurisdictions receive a format they recognize.

08Do I still need an Environmental Professional?

Yes. Barrow drafts; the EP reviews, applies professional judgment and signs off. The drafts are not final reports.

09What about data security?

Project files stay private to your account. Reach out for details on how your data is stored and handled.

10Why not just use ChatGPT?

Barrow is purpose-built for environmental reporting: it works with your source documents, follows ASTM structure and keeps facts consistent across a full-length report.
