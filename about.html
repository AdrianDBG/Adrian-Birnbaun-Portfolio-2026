About me /

Graphic, digital, web and UX/UI designer

Seven years designing for brands, screens, and the weird space in between. I came up through visual identity — logos, editorial, the kind of work where a single curve carries the whole message. That trained my eye. Then I moved toward interfaces, and I had to train everything else: how people read, how they move, where they hesitate, what they skip.

Now I do both, and I think that's where the work gets interesting. A brand that doesn't hold up at 16 pixels is a brand that wasn't finished. A product that looks right but feels wrong is a product nobody will explain to their friends. I'm interested in closing that gap.

Based in Buenos Aires

Working from Buenos Aires with clients I've never shared a timezone with. I freelance internationally because the constraint is the point — tight briefs, async communication, no room for ambiguity. It forces clarity, and clarity is most of the job. The rest is taste.
