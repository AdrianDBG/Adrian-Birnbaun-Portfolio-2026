About /

Loula is a LatAm-focused fintech platform simplifying international payments for importers, exporters, and service providers. Its core identity centers on efficient cross-border money movement, offering USD accounts, instant currency conversion (ARS, USD, USDC), global supplier payments, and trade finance. Live and expanding, Loula streamlines complex financial operations, empowering LatAm businesses in global commerce.

For this project, a complete brand identity was developed from the ground up, merging current design trends with a fresh perspective on the crypto and cross-border transaction world. The work included the creation of a modern visual system applied across high-conversion landing pages, social media assets, and large-scale corporate signage, ensuring a consistent and innovative brand experience that empowers Latin American businesses in the global market.
