About /

Located in the heart of the Palermo neighborhood, the Hipódromo de Palermo is a true icon of Buenos Aires and a symbol of Argentine horse racing. With over 140 years of history, it blends the elegance of its classical architecture with a vibrant social and cultural scene. Internationally renowned for its prestige, it is much more than a venue for top-level races — it's a space where tradition and innovation meet, offering unique experiences that go far beyond the sport itself.

As a Designer for one of the region's most iconic entertainment venues, the focus is on merging functionality with high-impact visuals. The work involves creating user-friendly landing pages that look great on any device and designing engaging email campaigns that connect with the audience. Additionally, a key part of the role is professionalizing the Casino department's internal image, from transforming complex data into clear, branded presentations to designing all the essential print materials and stationery used in daily operations.
